## 3. Risks and decision points

The risks specific to the site and to the model are recorded in Section 11 of [malta_valletta_model_plan.md](malta_valletta_model_plan.md). Those below concern the structure of the period itself and are independent of the site.

**Cost of fragmentation.** Each return imposes approximately half a day of recontextualisation, amounting over six blocks to some three working days. Mitigated by closing every block with a written handoff, following the discipline of the progress reports maintained in StagnoneDT.

**Collision between the UNIPA commitments and a Malta block.** Accepted rather than mitigated. Reactive work waits for the following interval unless a journal deadline precludes delay.

**Displacement of Stagnone Paper 1.** The manuscript is substantially complete and its remaining impediments are minor, but it has no external deadline compelling submission. Should G1 be consumed by other work the manuscript is liable to remain unsubmitted for the duration of the period. The allocation to G1 is therefore treated as fixed rather than indicative.

The risks arising from the field campaign, namely delay in authorisation, failure of the campaign and insufficiency of the bathymetry, have lapsed. No campaign is planned and the bathymetry has been obtained. The reasoning is preserved in Appendix C.

# Appendices: superseded material

The following records how the site and the campaign were framed before either was settled. It is retained for the record and is not current.

## Appendix A. Objective hierarchy as framed before site selection

The proposal of April 2026 opened three lines of enquiry. Across 86 fragmented working days these could not be pursued at equal depth and were therefore ranked. The ranking below was subsequently revised in the light of the site selection, and the current position is recorded in Section 8 of the Valletta plan.

**Principal objective.** Transfer of the coupled FM and SWAN framework to a Maltese site, together with measurement of the effort that transfer required. The effort itself constitutes the measurement. A structured record of what was reused unchanged, what required site-specific adjustment and what had to be written anew supplies the primary evidence for the reproducibility claim. In the absence of that record the thesis chapter rests on anecdote.

**Supporting objective.** Retraining and stress-testing of the Random-Forest bottom-class classifier on Maltese scenes using ground-truth gathered in October. This was anticipated as the natural joint contribution with the host group, as the input required by the vegetation drag formulation, and as the element most clearly drawing on the host group's expertise in remote sensing and machine learning.

**Bounded secondary analysis.** CALYPSO and i-WaveNET HF radar surface currents, cross-checked against the model and against the Stagnone offshore boundary. Being analysis of existing data rather than new model construction, this was expected to fit a single block and to degrade gracefully under compression.

**Reduced to a discussion point.** Vertical land movement within the long-term scenario layer. It was the weakest of the three lines in the proposal and the one without a path to a result inside the window.

## Appendix B. Site selection criteria

The site was the decision from which everything downstream followed. The criteria below were established before the decision, in order of veto power, and the outcome was the selection of the Valletta harbour system on 22 September 2026. Criterion 2 was not satisfied by the site selected, and the consequences of that are addressed in Section 1 of the Valletta plan.

1. **The connection to the sea must admit representation as an open boundary.** Where exchange occurs through a culvert or sluice the model becomes a problem in hydraulic structures and transferability ceases to be what is under test. This criterion excluded Il-Magħluq in April.
2. **Sub-metre to few-metre depth over a vegetated bed.** Without a canopy the `[veg]` module has no function and a substantial part of the framework stands idle.
3. **Scale materially different from the Stagnone without being degenerate.** A site five to fifty times smaller tests the transfer. A site a thousand times smaller becomes an exercise in mesh resolution rather than in physics.
4. **At least one water-level source independent of any campaign**, so that instrument failure would not remove the validation entirely.
5. **Access and authorisation achievable within three weeks.** Natura 2000 status implies a permit from the Environment and Resources Authority, and that lead time was the critical path of the campaign.
6. **Recognition as the WETWISE Maltese pilot.** A site not recognised by the project would not contribute to deliverable D.3.3.3, weakening the institutional case for the period.

The candidates put to the host group, none assumed, were Is-Salini and Salina Bay, Il-Ballut ta' Marsaxlokk as the default carried from April, and shallow vegetated embayments including Marsaxlokk, Mellieħa and St Paul's.

## Appendix C. Block plan as framed before site selection

The block content below assumed a vegetated lagoon site and a field campaign in October. Both assumptions have lapsed. The current block allocation is Section 10 of the Valletta plan. The campaign design is retained here because the priority ordering would apply again should a limited deployment subsequently prove necessary.

**Campaign products, in fixed priority order**, the ordering being determined by the fact that weather governs how many can be completed.

1. **Pressure loggers.** Two or three submerged together with one barometric reference ashore, moored within the first two days and left until March, with partial downloads in December and January. This would have constituted the dataset validating everything else.
2. **Habitat ground-truth.** Sixty to a hundred and twenty GPS points recorded with a drop-camera, stratified by class. Inexpensive, requiring no large vessel, and supplying the classifier retraining.
3. **Bathymetry.** Single-beam echo sounder with RTK-GNSS from a kayak along parallel lines over two to three days, with satellite-derived bathymetry as the fallback where the water is sufficiently clear and shallow.
4. **GPS drifters.** A repetition of the Stagnone protocol, requiring one or two days of usable wind, and the element that would have rendered the Maltese results directly comparable with Paper 1.
5. **CTD profiles.** Stratification, initial condition and validation.

**Associated risks, now lapsed.** Delay in authorisation was the dependency on which the whole plan rested, mitigated by filing on 25 September and by holding a fallback site without Natura 2000 restrictions. Insufficiency of the bathymetry would have produced a mesh incapable of calibration, which is why the survey stood third in the campaign priority. Outright failure of the campaign would have reduced the joint manuscript to framework transferability on public data alone, which remains the reason the porting log rather than the campaign was designated the principal objective.
